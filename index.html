ℹ️ How QuizBee Works

🐝 Welcome to QuizBee

QuizBee is a skill-based gaming platform where you use QuizBee Points to enter games and compete for prizes.

🪙 1. QuizBee Points

QuizBee Points are used to enter games and participate in activities on QuizBee.

They are separate from your Prize Balance.

🎮 2. Play Games

Choose an available game, pay its entry fee using QuizBee Points and play the challenge.

Different games have different rules, challenges and winning conditions.

🏆 3. Win Prizes

When you win an eligible competition, your prize is added to your Prize Balance.

Prize Balance is separate from your QuizBee Points.

💸 4. Withdraw Your Prize

You can request a withdrawal from your Prize Balance using the available payment methods.

Withdrawals are reviewed and processed by QuizBee team.

📺 5. Earn Points From Ads

Watch eligible rewarded advertisements and receive QuizBee Points after the advertisement is successfully completed.

👥 6. Invite Friends

Share your personal referral link with friends.

Referral attribution is recorded when a new user joins QuizBee through your referral link.

🔥 7. Keep Playing

QuizBee also tracks your game participation streak.

Your streak increases through successful new game participation and you stand a chance to win a huge prize from having the highest streak for the week/month, same applies to referrals.

🐝 Easy to understand. Fun to play. Lots of prizes to win.
